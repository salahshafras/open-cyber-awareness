📱 What is SMS Smishing?

Smishing (SMS + Phishing) is when criminals send fraudulent text messages to trick you into revealing personal information, clicking malicious links, or sending money.

🏢 Example: NexaTrust

Imagine you work at NexaTrust. A smishing message might:

  • Claim to be from "NexaTrust Security" but come from an unknown number
  • Create panic: "Your account will be suspended in 1 hour!"
  • Include a suspicious link like nexatrust-secure.com or nexatrust.verify-now.com
  • Ask you to "confirm your identity" or "verify payment information"
  • Request you to reply with sensitive codes or passwords

🛡️ How to Spot SMS Smishing:

  • Check the sender number - legitimate companies use verified short codes
  • Look for urgency tactics - "Act now!" or "Last chance!"
  • Inspect links carefully - hover or long-press to see the real URL
  • Watch for spelling errors in company names or messages
  • Never click links in unsolicited texts - go directly to the official app or website
  • Don't reply to suspicious messages - this confirms your number is active
