📧 What is Email Phishing?

Email phishing is when criminals send fake emails pretending to be from trusted organizations to steal your sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

🏢 Example: NexaTrust.com

Imagine you work at NexaTrust.com, a financial services company. A phishing email might:

  • Claim to be from "NexaTrust IT Support" but use a fake email address
  • Create urgency: "Your account will be locked in 24 hours!"
  • Ask you to click a link to "verify your password"
  • Lead to a fake website that looks like nexatrust.com but is actually nexatrust-verify.com

🛡️ How to Spot Email Phishing:

  • Check the sender's email address carefully
  • Look for spelling mistakes and poor grammar
  • Hover over links before clicking to see the real URL
  • Be suspicious of urgent requests for passwords or personal info
  • Verify independently - contact the company directly if unsure
