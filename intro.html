Welcome to Phishing Awareness Training.

Phishing is one of the most common and damaging cyber threats today. Criminals send fake emails, SMS, or even make phone calls to trick people into giving away passwords, one-time codes, or financial information. These attacks can look real, using company logos, urgent wording, or fake websites to pressure you into acting quickly.

The goal of this training is simple: help you recognize phishing attempts before they cause harm. Through interactive scenarios, you will practice spotting suspicious messages and making safe choices in a risk-free environment.

What You’ll Experience

  • Review realistic examples of emails, SMS, and phone call scenarios.
  • Learn to check sender details, links, attachments, and wording.
  • Decide whether to Trust or Report.
  • Receive instant feedback on your choice, with clear explanations.
  • Finish with a short awareness check.

Why It Matters

One click on a phishing link can open the door to stolen data, financial loss, or even a company-wide breach. By building these skills, you’ll protect not only yourself but also your organization.

What’s Included

  • Email phishing: fake HR notices, invoices, password resets.
  • SMS / Smishing: text scams with urgent payment or delivery requests.
  • Voice phishing (Vishing): how callers trick people (covered in scenario text and quizzes).
  • Best practices: what to do when something looks suspicious.
