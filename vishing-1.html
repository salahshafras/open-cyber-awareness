📞 What is Voice Phishing (Vishing)?

Vishing (Voice + Phishing) is when criminals use phone calls to impersonate trusted organizations and manipulate you into revealing sensitive information, transferring money, or granting system access.

🏢 Example: NexaTrust.com

Imagine you work at NexaTrust.com. A vishing call might:

  • Claim to be from "NexaTrust IT Security" using a spoofed caller ID
  • Create extreme urgency: "Your account has been hacked! We need to act NOW!"
  • Demand passwords, MFA codes, or employee credentials immediately
  • Threaten consequences: "If you don't comply, you'll lose access to everything"
  • Use background noise to sound like a real call center
  • Pressure you not to hang up or verify through other channels

🛡️ How to Spot Voice Phishing:

  • Verify caller identity independently - hang up and call back using official numbers
  • Never share passwords or codes - legitimate IT never asks for these over the phone
  • Be wary of high-pressure tactics - scammers use fear and urgency
  • Question unexpected calls - especially about security issues or account problems
  • Don't trust caller ID alone - it can be easily spoofed
  • Take your time - real companies will allow you to verify and call back
